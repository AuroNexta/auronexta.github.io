Vision Forecast v2 — 21% MAPE Reduction

📅 09 August 2025 ⏱ 12 min read ✍ AuroNexta AI/ML Team

Our temporal transformer pipeline now covers 200+ signals with automated retraining via CI/CD, delivering a 21% MAPE reduction over our legacy forecasting system. This update marks a major milestone in our mission to bring production-grade AI to every business.

The Challenge

Our client, a multi-national retail and manufacturing group, struggled with demand forecasting across 400+ SKUs and 12 distribution centres. Their legacy ARIMA-based system suffered from high MAPE (32% average), manual model tuning, and no real-time response to demand shocks like supply chain disruptions or seasonal spikes.

The result? Overstock in some locations, stock-outs in others, and $2.1M in annual inventory holding costs that could have been avoided with more accurate predictions.

Our Approach

We built a multi-horizon demand forecasting pipeline from the ground up using PyTorch temporal transformers. The system ingests 200+ signals including:

  • Historical sales data (5+ years, daily granularity)
  • Weather forecasts and seasonal indices
  • Promotional calendars and price elasticity
  • Supplier lead-time variability
  • Macroeconomic indicators (inflation, consumer confidence)
  • Competitor pricing signals (web-scraped)

Architecture

The pipeline is built for production, not experimentation. Every component is containerised, monitored, and auto-retrained:

  1. Data Ingestion Layer — Apache Kafka streams real-time sales and external signals into a Delta Lake on AWS S3
  2. Feature Store — 200+ engineered features with automated drift detection and alerting
  3. Model Layer — Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) with graph neural networks for store clustering, ensembled with ARIMA baselines
  4. Serving Layer — SageMaker endpoints (3 replicas, auto-scaling) with <200ms p99 latency
  5. CI/CD Pipeline — Airflow DAGs trigger nightly retraining; new models promoted via canary deployment after automated evaluation
