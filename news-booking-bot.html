Booking Bot Goes Live for European Client

📅 22 July 2025 ⏱ 10 min read ✍ AuroNexta Agentic Systems Team

24/7 autonomous bookings with WhatsApp integration — 38% conversion uplift from abandoned flows. Our agentic booking system is now live for a leading European service provider.

The Challenge

Our client operates across multiple European time zones and struggled with missed bookings during off-hours, long queue wait times, and high operational costs for their call centre. Manual booking processes were slow, error-prone, and limited to business hours — resulting in lost revenue and frustrated customers.

Our Solution: The Booking Bot

We designed an autonomous agentic chatbot that handles end-to-end bookings, scheduling, and confirmations without human intervention. The system integrates seamlessly with WhatsApp and a web widget, providing a natural conversational interface that customers already know and trust.

Architecture

  • LLM Planner: GPT-4 with tool-calling pipeline for intent parsing and slot filling
  • Calendar Integration: Real-time availability checking across multiple service providers
  • Payment Gateway: Stripe integration for deposits and full payments
  • Human Handoff: Seamless escalation to live agents with full conversation context

Data Flow

  1. User initiates conversation via WhatsApp or web widget
  2. Agent parses intent, checks real-time availability
  3. Books slot, sends confirmation with deep-link and calendar invite
  4. Follows up 24h before appointment with reminder and reschedule option
  5. Post-appointment feedback collection for continuous improvement

Technical Stack

The system is built on Python and FastAPI with Redis for session management and PostgreSQL for the bookings table. Stripe and Twilio webhooks handle payments and messaging respectively. The entire platform is containerised and deployed on AWS with auto-scaling for peak demand periods.

What's Next

We're now expanding the system to handle multi-language support (currently English, German, and French) and adding predictive scheduling — using historical data to proactively suggest optimal booking times based on customer preferences and service availability.
