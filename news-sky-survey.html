Sky Survey Completes Railway Corridor Mapping

📅 01 August 2025 ⏱ 10 min read ✍ AuroNexta Drone Operations

A 120km BVLOS corridor has been successfully inspected with 4cm GSD orthomosaics and automated damage detection — cutting inspection costs by 50% and eliminating track closures for ground teams.

The Challenge

Railway corridor inspection traditionally requires ground teams walking the full length of track — a process that is slow, dangerous, and requires partial track closures. Our client needed a way to inspect 120km of active rail corridor without disrupting service or putting staff at risk.

Our Solution: Sky Survey

We deployed our BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) drone fleet to conduct a comprehensive corridor survey. The operation covered 120km across mixed terrain — urban, suburban, and rural segments — in a single 5-day window.

Fleet Configuration

  • Platform: DJI Matrice 300 RTK with redundant power system
  • Camera: Zenmuse P1 — 45MP Phase One full-frame camera
  • LiDAR: DJI Zenmuse L1 for point cloud registration
  • Flight Planning: Automated via Pix4Dcapture with dynamic waypoint optimisation
  • Telemetry: Real-time dashboard with live video, GPS, and battery monitoring

Processing Pipeline

  1. Image Capture: 85% forward / 60% side lap at 120m AGL → 4cm GSD orthomosaics
  2. Cloud Processing: Agisoft Metashape with GPU-accelerated bundle adjustment
  3. LiDAR Registration: Point cloud aligned to orthomosaic for 3D accuracy validation
  4. Automated Detection: CNN-based crack and damage detection trained on 50K+ labelled images

Beyond Railways

The same Sky Survey system has been deployed for solar farm inspections and pipeline integrity monitoring. Our BVLOS certification and automated detection pipeline make this solution applicable to any linear infrastructure — power lines, waterways, and highways.
